The OJAS (Online Job Application System) portal is the official recruitment platform for Gujarat government jobs — GPSC, GPSSSB, GSSSB, and all state-level recruitments. Every application requires a photo and signature upload with strict specifications.

## OJAS Photo Specifications

| Parameter | Specification |
|-----------|--------------|
| Dimensions | 150 × 200 pixels |
| File size | Max 15 KB |
| Format | JPG / JPEG |
| Background | Plain white |

OJAS has one of the **strictest photo caps** — only 15KB, tighter than SSC (20KB).

## OJAS Signature Specifications

| Parameter | Specification |
|-----------|--------------|
| Dimensions | 140 × 60 pixels |
| File size | Max 15 KB |
| Format | JPG / JPEG |

## How to Prepare Your OJAS Photo and Signature

### Photo
1. Stand against a plain white wall in daylight.
2. Use the [Custom Size tool](/tool/custom-resize) and enter "150x200 px, max 15kb".
3. Or use the [Compress to 15KB tool](/tool/compress-15kb) after resizing.

### Signature
1. Sign on plain white paper with a black pen.
2. Use the [Signature Resize tool](/tool/signature-resize) to crop to 140×60px.
3. Use the [Compress to 15KB tool](/tool/compress-15kb) to bring under 15KB.

## Why OJAS Has a 15KB Cap

The OJAS portal was built for low-bandwidth rural Gujarat. The 15KB cap ensures the portal can handle thousands of simultaneous uploads during recruitment windows without server overload. While the cap is strict, the 150×200px dimension is small enough that 15KB preserves adequate facial detail.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the OJAS photo size?** 150×200px, JPG, max 15KB, white background.
**What is the OJAS signature size?** 140×60px, JPG, max 15KB.
**Can I use the same photo for OJAS and SSC?** No — OJAS requires 15KB max, SSC allows 20KB. The dimensions are also different (150×200 vs 200×230).
