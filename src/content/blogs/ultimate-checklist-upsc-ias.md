The **Union Public Service Commission (UPSC)** Civil Services Exam is widely known as one of the most prestigious and challenging competitive exams in the world. Aspirants dedicate years to studying standard reference texts, current affairs, and answer-writing formulas to clear the selection filters.

Yet, many candidates fail at the very first step: **the online registration flow.**

The UPSC portal enforces strict, automated checks on candidate photographs, digital signatures, and PDF identity card uploads. If any file deviates from these rules, the upload fails or leads to cancellation during manual review.

In this ultimate manual, we break down the definitive checklist you must follow to get your UPSC application papers accepted without delay.

---

## 1. UPSC Official Document Rules

UPSC requires three distinct document uploads during the registration process:

### Candidate Photograph
* **File Size:** Must be within **20 KB to 300 KB**.
* **Preferred Dimensions:** Minimum **350 pixels width by 350 pixels height** to a maximum of **1000 pixels by 1000 pixels**. Both dimensions must be equal (square, 1:1 aspect ratio).
* **Face Area:** Your face must cover at least **75%** of the photographic area.
* **Important Guidelines:** Background must be clean white or extremely light. No spectacles with tint or glare, no caps, and your gaze must be centered.

### Candidate Signature
* **File Size:** Must be within **20 KB to 300 KB**.
* **Preferred Dimensions:** Minimum **350 pixels width by 350 pixels height** to a maximum of **1000 pixels by 1000 pixels**.
* **Guidelines:** Sign in cursive using a thick black gel ink pen on a plain white unruled paper page.

### Identity Card PDF
* **File Size:** Must be within **20 KB to 300 KB**.
* **Format:** Strictly PDF format.
* **Content:** This must match the document number specified during the form entry (Aadhaar Card, Driving License, Voter ID, PAN Card, etc.).

---

## 2. Setting Up Square UPSC Photos

Unlike other boards that favor rectangular passport layouts (e.g. 3.5cm x 4.5cm), UPSC mandates a **square 1:1 layout** for both photos and signatures.

If you try to upload a standard rectangular photo, the portal stretches the canvas, warping your proportions. Ensure you crop your candidate files to a strict square layout beforehand.
