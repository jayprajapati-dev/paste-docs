You have filled out all details on the registration form, paid the application fee, and got your registration number copy. But when exam cards are issued, you receive a rejection notice: **"Application Rejected: Photo Not as Per Specifications."**

This is the worst nightmare of any civil service, banking, or railway aspirant. Every year, nearly **10% to 15% of all candidates** are disqualified because their photos failed basic verification checks.

Let's look at the 5 most common mistakes candidates make and how you should avoid them to ensure 100% acceptance.

---

## The 5 Fatal Mistakes to Avoid

### 1. Wearing Spectacles with Glare
While some portals allow reading prescription glasses in regular life, almost all automated photo checkers reject images if there is any flash glare or reflection on the lenses. Glare blocks your iris and pupils, making face authorization impossible.

> **Pro Tip:** Play it safe. Take off your spectacles entirely when getting your passport photo clicked. None of the major central boards (UPSC, SSC, IBPS, NTA) will reject your photo for not having glasses, but they *will* reject it for glass glare.

### 2. Spliced or Textured Backgrounds
A lot of applicants use selfies taken in a garden, or crop a group picture where curtains or tiles are visible in the background. Official rules demand a **completely uniform, solid background (white or light blue).**

If there is any color gradient, pattern, or shadow, automated checkers will reject the image for failing background clarity scans.

### 3. Wearing Caps, Mufflers, or Large Ornaments
Your head must be fully exposed. Avoid wearing hats, caps, active headbands, or excessively large earrings/nose rings. 

*Exception:* Religious wear like turbans or hijabs are permitted by most boards, but your face must be completely uncovered from the hair-line to the tip of your chin.

### 4. Poor Contrast and Low-Resolution Mobile Crops
If you zoom in and crop your face from a large landscape image, the resolution drops significantly. The face appears pixelated, blurry, and out of focus. Automated facial matching engines will fail to authenticate your facial features.

### 5. Squeezing or Stretching the Image (Ratio Mismatch)
When portals ask for specific dimensions like **3.5cm x 4.5cm**, and you use basic tools to squash a wide picture, it distorts your facial features. Manual auditors identify this immediately as mismatch.

## How to Check If Your Photo is Valid

Before uploading, do a quick checklist check:
* **Background:** Is it solid white or solid light blue?
* **Ears:** Are both of your ears fully visible?
* **Gaze:** Are you looking straight into the camera lens with neutral alignment?
* **Size:** Does the KB file size fall strictly within the allowed range?
* **Clarity:** Can you see fine details when zooming in?
