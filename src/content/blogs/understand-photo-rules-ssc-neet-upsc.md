Filing an application form for competitive exam boards in India is a massive milestone. Whether you're aiming for recruitment through the **Staff Selection Commission (SSC)**, seeking a professional track in medicine with **NEET**, or pursuing civil service via the **UPSC**, you face a labyrinth of guidelines.

The number one reason for application rejection in India is **improper photo or signature formatting**. In fact, every year, tens of thousands of students are disqualified before they even step foot into the exam hall simply because of minor formatting discrepancies.

In this cheat sheet, we break down the latest photo and document rules for the top exam boards and explain how to satisfy them securely.

---

## The Big Three Cheat Sheet

### 1. Staff Selection Commission (SSC)
* **Photo Size:** Strictly between **20 KB to 50 KB**.
* **Dimensions:** **3.5 cm (width) x 4.5 cm (height)** (equivalent to **200 x 230 pixels**).
* **Background:** Light background, preferably plain white.
* **Important Rule:** Do NOT wear a cap or spectacles. Your eyes, nose, and ears must be fully visible. 

### 2. National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET - NTA)
* **Passport Photo Size:** **10 KB to 200 KB**.
* **Postcard Photo (4x6):** **10 KB to 200 KB**.
* **Background:** Strictly plain white.
* **Important Rule:** The candidate's name and the date of taking the photograph must be printed at the bottom of the photo. No caps, glasses, or shadows are permitted.

### 3. Union Public Service Commission (UPSC)
* **Photo Size:** Between **20 KB to 300 KB**.
* **Dimensions:** **350 x 350 pixels (minimum)** to **1000 x 1000 pixels (maximum)**.
* **Background:** Plain white.
* **Important Rule:** Up to 3/4th (75%) of the image space should represent your face in a close-up.

## 5 Golden Rules of Exam Portals

Before uploading any photo, make sure it satisfies these 5 critical rules:

1. **Rule of Ears:** Both your ears must be clearly visible. If your hair covers your ears, automated portrait algorithms will fail to calculate your face symmetry and reject the image.
2. **Spectacles Glare:** While some portals allow standard reading glasses, any reflection or flash glare on the lens will invalidate the photo. It’s highly recommended to take your photo without glasses.
3. **No Selfies:** Only upload professional studio portraits or crisp passport photos taken by a second person. Selfies cause perspective facial warping.
4. **Dates & Names:** If a portal requires your name and the date printed at the bottom (like NEET), it must be crisp, legible, and non-digitized.
5. **No Blur:** High compression often results in "feathering" around the hair and shoulders. Keep the compression level high-fidelity.
