iPhones default to HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) format to save storage. But virtually every Indian government portal, exam form, and visa application rejects HEIC and requires JPG. If you try to upload an iPhone photo, you will get a "invalid file format" error.

## Why Government Portals Reject HEIC

HEIC is a modern format (since iOS 11) that produces files about half the size of JPEG. However:
- Most government portals were built before HEIC existed
- The portals validate file extensions and only accept .jpg/.jpeg
- HEIC support in server-side image libraries is inconsistent

## How to Stop iPhone from Taking HEIC Photos

If you want to avoid the conversion step entirely:
1. Open Settings → Camera → Formats
2. Change from "High Efficiency" to "Most Compatible"
3. Your iPhone will now shoot JPG instead of HEIC

However, this doubles your photo file size. The better approach is to keep HEIC for everyday use and use PasteDocs to convert when you need to upload to a portal.
