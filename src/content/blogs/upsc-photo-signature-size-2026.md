The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) requires a photo and signature upload for every exam application — Civil Services (CSE), CDS, NDA, CAPF, and all other UPSC recruitments. The specifications are consistent across UPSC exams.

## UPSC Photo Specifications

| Parameter | Specification |
|-----------|--------------|
| Dimensions | 200 × 230 pixels (passport size) |
| File size | 20 KB to 50 KB (minimum 20KB, maximum 50KB) |
| Format | JPG / JPEG |
| Background | Plain white |

Note the **minimum of 20KB** — if your photo is under 20KB, UPSC rejects it as too low quality.

## UPSC Signature Specifications

| Parameter | Specification |
|-----------|--------------|
| Dimensions | 140 × 60 pixels |
| File size | 10 KB to 20 KB |
| Format | JPG / JPEG |
| Background | Plain white |

## How to Prepare Your UPSC Photo and Signature

### Photo
1. Stand against a plain white wall in daylight.
2. Use the [Passport Size Photo tool](/tool/passport-size) to crop to 413×531px.
3. Use the [Compress to 50KB tool](/tool/compress-50kb) to bring under 50KB.
4. Verify the file is between 20KB and 50KB.

### Signature
1. Sign on plain white paper with a black/dark blue pen.
2. Use the [Signature Resize tool](/tool/signature-resize) to crop to 140×60px.
3. Use the [Compress to 20KB tool](/tool/compress-20kb) to bring under 20KB.
4. Verify the file is between 10KB and 20KB.

## UPSC Photo Card Requirements

For the UPSC CSE Mains exam, you must also carry a printed photo card. The photo on the card must match the uploaded photo. NTA recommends printing the photo on standard photo paper and carrying 2-3 copies.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the UPSC photo size?**
200×230 pixels, JPG, 20-50KB, white background.

**What is the UPSC signature size?**
140×60 pixels, JPG, 10-20KB.

**Can I use the same photo for UPSC CSE and CDS?**
Yes — both have the same specification (200×230px, 20-50KB).

No. PasteDocs processes in your browser. Your photo never leaves your device.
