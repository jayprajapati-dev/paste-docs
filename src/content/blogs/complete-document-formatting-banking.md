Filing an online application for nationalized Indian banks can feel like a part-time job in itself. Major recruiting boards like the **Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS)**, the **State Bank of India (SBI)**, and the **Reserve Bank of India (RBI)** have incredibly complex upload flows.

Unlike other examinations that only ask for a photo and a signature, banking exams require up to **four different document uploads**:
1. Candidate Photograph
2. Candidate Signature
3. Left Thumb Impression (LTI)
4. Handwritten Declaration (in English only)

If a single one of these fails the file size or aspect checks, your application can be cancelled or rejected. Let's break down each of these rules step-by-step.

---

## 1. Banking Exam Document Checklist

Here are the strict, updated guidelines for IBPS, SBI, and RBI recruitment portals:

### Candidate Photograph
* **Size:** Must be between **20 KB to 50 KB**.
* **Dimensions:** **200 x 230 pixels** (preferred format).
* **Color:** High contrast against a light, preferably plain white background. No specs reflections or hats.

### Candidate Signature
* **Size:** Must be between **10 KB to 20 KB**.
* **Dimensions:** **140 x 60 pixels**.
* **Important Rule:** Do NOT use CAPITAL letters for your signature. Portals flag signatures in block caps as invalid. Always sign in cursive black ink.

### Left Thumb Impression (LTI)
* **Size:** Must be between **20 KB to 50 KB**.
* **Dimensions:** **240 x 240 pixels** (approximate aspect ratio 1:1, square).
* **Ink Color:** Blue or black ink stamp pads. The thumbprint ridges must be clear and unsmudged.

### Handwritten Declaration
* **Size:** Must be between **50 KB to 100 KB**.
* **Dimensions:** **800 x 400 pixels** (approximate aspect ratio 2:1).
* **Language:** Written strictly in English. Do NOT write in capital block letters. Writing must be in neat, standard cursive scripts using black ink on unruled white paper.

---

## 2. Setting Up Each Document Perfectly

### The Left Thumb Impression (LTI)
Press your left thumb onto a standard office ink pad. Lightly press it onto a piece of unruled white paper. Ensure the ridges are distinct and not a solid blob of ink. Take a well-lit photo using your phone and crop it closely to a square.

### The Handwritten Declaration
The text for the declaration must contain the exact words outlined in the official exam notification. Generally, it says:

> "I, _______ (Name of candidate), hereby declare that all the information submitted by me in the application form is correct, true, and valid. I will present the supporting documents as and when required."

Write this in your normal cursive handwriting using black ink on a blank page. Photograph it, keeping borders clean, and crop it to wide rectangle dimensions.
