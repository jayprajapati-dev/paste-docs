Preparing for the Staff Selection Commission (SSC) exams like CGL, CHSL, or GD is an intense journey. You spend months studying quantitative aptitude, reasoning, English, and general awareness. But when the application window opens, many aspirants run into a massive roadblock: **uploading their photograph and signature.**

The SSC has notoriously strict guidelines. If your photo is even 1KB over the limit, or if the pixels are off by a single margin, the server rejects it. In worse cases, the application accepts the file, but it gets rejected during manual checking weeks later, causing you to lose your chance to sit for the exam.

In this guide, we will walk you through the exact steps to resize your photo and signature to meet the SSC CGL and CHSL limits of **20KB–50KB for photos** and **10KB–20KB for signatures**—securely and in seconds.

---

## 1. Demystifying the Strict SSC Guidelines

Let's first look at the official board requirements for SSC notifications:

### For the Photograph:
* **Format:** Strictly **JPG** or **JPEG**.
* **File Size:** Must be within **20 KB to 50 KB**.
* **Dimensions:** **3.5 cm (width) x 4.5 cm (height)**. In digital screen pixels, this translates to **200 pixels width by 230 pixels height**.
* **Recency:** The photo must be clicked within 3 months of the notification release date.
* **Background:** Plain white or light blue.

### For the Signature:
* **Format:** Strictly **JPG** or **JPEG**.
* **File Size:** Must be within **10 KB to 20 KB**.
* **Dimensions:** **4.0 cm (width) x 2.0 cm (height)**. In digital screen pixels, this translates to **140 pixels width by 60 pixels height**.
* **Contrast:** Black ink on static white paper.

---

## 2. Common Challenges faced by SSC Aspirants

Why do so many students fail this upload step?

* **Raw Photo Sizes:** Photos clicked by your phone's front camera are naturally 3MB to 8MB in size, while the portal requires less than 50KB.
* **Aspect Ratio Distortion:** When trying to resize using simple mobile editors, the photo gets squeezed or stretched. If your facial proportions look distorted, automated verification filters flag the photo during audit.
* **Dashed lines or Grids:** Cropping a signature often clips the stroke ends, resulting in an incomplete signature file that triggers a mismatch during exams.

## 3. How to Resize Your SSC Photo block-by-block

Let's break down how you can prepare these files manually (or automated) safely:

### Step 1: Click the Perfect Raw Photo
Stand against a solid plain white wall during midday so there are no shadows behind your head. Keep your shoulders level, look straight into the camera, and make sure both ears are fully visible. Keep your facial expression neutral.

### Step 2: Use Black Ink for Your Signature
On a clean unruled sheet of paper, draw a neat box measuring roughly 4cm x 2cm. Sign inside that box using a thick black gel or fountain pen. This ensures high-contrast lines that automated cropping tools can isolate easily.

### Step 3: Resizing and Compression (The Struggle)
Usually, students try to download offline apps or visit shady compression sites. This is highly risky.

> **Warning:** Never upload photos holding your identity documents (or photos with highly personal credentials) to online crop utilities that process files on external servers. Identity theft and leak loops are common with unsecured image hosts.
