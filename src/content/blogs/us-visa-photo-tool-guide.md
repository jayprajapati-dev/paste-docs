The US visa application (DS-160 form) requires a digital photograph that meets strict specifications set by the US Department of State. If the photo does not meet the spec, the DS-160 form will reject it — and you will need to retake and re-upload before you can submit the application.

This guide covers the exact US visa photo requirements for 2026, the most common rejection reasons, and how to resize your photo to the correct specification using a free tool that runs entirely in your browser.

## US Visa Photo Specifications (DS-160 Form)

The US Department of State specifies the following requirements for the digital visa photo:

| Parameter | Specification |
|-----------|--------------|
| Dimensions | 600 × 600 pixels (square) |
| File size | 54 KB to 240 KB (recommended under 240 KB) |
| Format | JPG / JPEG |
| Background | Plain white, uniform, no patterns |
| Head position | Front-facing, centred, both eyes open |
| Head size | 50% to 69% of the total photo height (approximately 1 inch to 1-3/8 inches from chin to crown) |
| Resolution | 300 DPI minimum (at 600×600 px this is native) |

The photo must be in colour (24 bits per pixel), taken within the last 6 months, and with no glasses (the US removed the glasses exception in November 2016).

## Common US Visa Photo Rejection Reasons

1. **Wrong dimensions** — the photo must be exactly 600×600 pixels (square). Rectangular photos are rejected. Use the US Visa Photo tool to apply the exact square crop.
2. **File size outside 54-240KB** — the DS-160 form has both a minimum (54KB) and maximum (240KB). Use the compress tools to land within the range.
3. **Wearing glasses** — the US removed the glasses exception in 2016. Remove glasses before taking the photo, even if you wear them daily.
4. **Background not white** — the background must be plain white with no patterns, shadows or textures. Use the [Add White Background tool](/tool/white-background) if your background is off-white.
5. **Head size incorrect** — the head (from chin to crown) must occupy 50-69% of the photo height. If your head is too small or too large in the frame, re-take the photo at the correct distance.
6. **Smiling or unnatural expression** — the US requires a neutral expression with both eyes open. Avoid wide smiles.
7. **Photo older than 6 months** — the photo must be recent. If your appearance has changed significantly, take a new photo.
