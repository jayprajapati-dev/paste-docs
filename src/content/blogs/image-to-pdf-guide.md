Many government portals require document uploads in PDF format only. If you photographed your Aadhaar, PAN, marksheet or certificate, you have a JPG — not a PDF. You need to convert it.

## How to Convert Image to PDF

Use the [Image to PDF tool](/tool/image-to-pdf) to convert JPG, PNG, HEIC or WEBP images to PDF entirely in your browser. The tool uses the open-source pdf-lib library — no upload to any server.

### Single image to PDF
Upload one image → get a single-page PDF.

### Multiple images to one PDF
Upload multiple images → get a multi-page PDF (one image per page, in upload order). Use the [Merge Images to PDF tool](/tool/merge-images-to-pdf) for this.

### Compress the PDF after conversion
If the portal has a file-size cap (common: 100KB, 200KB, 500KB), use the [Compress PDF tool](/tool/pdf-compress) after conversion.

## Common Use Cases

- **Aadhaar upload**: Photograph both sides → merge to one PDF → compress to portal cap
- **PAN card upload**: Photograph front → convert to PDF → compress
- **Marksheet upload**: Photograph each page → merge to one PDF → compress
- **Income certificate**: Photograph → convert to PDF → compress

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I convert JPG to PDF?** Use the PasteDocs Image to PDF tool. Upload, convert, download — 100% in your browser.
**Can I merge multiple images into one PDF?** Yes — use the Merge Images to PDF tool. Upload in order, get a multi-page PDF.
**Can I compress the PDF after conversion?** Yes — use the Compress PDF tool to meet portal file-size caps.
**Is my document uploaded to a server?** No. pdf-lib runs entirely in your browser. Your document never leaves your device.
