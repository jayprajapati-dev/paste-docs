Passport photo dimensions vary by country. If you are applying for an Indian passport, a US visa, a UK visa, or a Schengen visa, each has different pixel and dimension requirements. This guide covers them all.

## Indian Passport Photo

The Indian passport photo is **413×531 pixels** (35×45mm at 96 DPI), JPG format, with a maximum file size of 200KB. Use the [Passport Size Photo tool](/tool/passport-size) to apply the exact spec.

## US Visa Photo (DS-160)

The US visa photo is **600×600 pixels** (2×2 inch square), JPG, 54-240KB. This is the only major country that uses a **square** format. Use the [US Visa Photo tool](/visa/us-visa).

## UK and Schengen Visa Photo

UK and Schengen visa photos are both **413×531 pixels** (35×45mm), the same as the Indian passport. The UK accepts light grey or white background; Schengen accepts white or light grey. Use the [Passport Size Photo tool](/tool/passport-size) and then the [Add White Background tool](/tool/white-background).

## How to Take a Compliant Passport Photo at Home

1. Stand against a plain white wall in daylight
2. Face the camera directly with both ears visible
3. Neutral expression, both eyes open
4. No glasses, hats, or accessories
5. Use your phone's rear camera (not selfie)
6. Use the [Passport Photo Maker tool](/tool/passport-photo-maker) to crop to the correct country spec
