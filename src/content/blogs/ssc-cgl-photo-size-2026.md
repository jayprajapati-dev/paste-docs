The Staff Selection Commission Combined Graduate Level (SSC CGL) exam requires every candidate to upload a photograph and a scanned signature as part of the online application. Both must meet strict specifications — if either fails, the application is rejected.

This guide covers the exact SSC CGL 2026 photo and signature specifications and how to prepare both in under 5 minutes.

## SSC CGL 2026 Photo Specifications

| Parameter | Specification |
|-----------|--------------|
| Dimensions | 200 × 230 pixels (approximately 3.5 × 4 cm) |
| File size | 1 KB to 20 KB (strict — even 21KB is rejected) |
| Format | JPG / JPEG |
| Background | Plain white |

SSC has the **strictest photo cap among major Indian exams** — only 20KB, compared to 50KB for UPSC/IBPS and 200KB for NEET.

## SSC CGL 2026 Signature Specifications

| Parameter | Specification |
|-----------|--------------|
| Dimensions | 140 × 60 pixels |
| File size | 1 KB to 20 KB |
| Format | JPG / JPEG |
| Background | Plain white (sign on white paper) |

## How to Prepare Your SSC CGL Photo and Signature

### Photo
1. Stand against a plain white wall in daylight. Face the camera directly.
2. Use the [Passport Size Photo tool](/tool/passport-size) to crop to passport dimensions.
3. Use the [Compress to 20KB tool](/tool/compress-20kb) to bring the file under SSC's strict 20KB cap.
4. Verify the file is between 1KB and 20KB.

### Signature
1. Sign on plain white paper with a black or dark blue pen.
2. Photograph in daylight.
3. Use the [Signature Resize tool](/tool/signature-resize) to crop to 140×60px.
4. Use the [Compress to 20KB tool](/tool/compress-20kb) to bring under 20KB.

## Common SSC CGL Rejection Reasons

1. **File size over 20KB** — the #1 rejection. Use the iterative Compress to 20KB tool.
2. **Wrong dimensions** — use the dedicated tools for exact pixel sizes.
3. **Background not white** — use the [Add White Background tool](/tool/white-background).
4. **PNG format instead of JPG** — use the [PNG to JPG converter](/tool/png-to-jpg).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the SSC CGL photo size?**
200×230 pixels, JPG format, 1-20KB file size, white background.

**What is the SSC CGL signature size?**
140×60 pixels, JPG format, 1-20KB file size.

**Can I use the same photo for SSC and IBPS?**
No — SSC caps at 20KB, IBPS caps at 50KB. Compress separately for each exam.
